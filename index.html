About

I work on compact ultrafast solid-state lasers: mode-locked bulk lasers and femtosecond-laser-inscribed waveguide lasers, from continuous-wave to Q-switched and mode-locked operation. My background also includes quasi-phase-matched nonlinear optics, precision thin-film metrology, and numerical modeling of pulse propagation. More on my research →
