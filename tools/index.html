Tools

Free browser-based calculators for physicists and engineers working with ultrafast lasers, nonlinear optics, and thin films. Everything runs locally in your browser.

For laser physicists

Pulse fluence and peak power calculator

Pulse energy, fluence, and peak power of pulsed lasers.

Ultrafast pulse parameter calculator

Pulse duration and bandwidth based on time-bandwidth products.

Gaussian beam focusing

Ideal Gaussian beam focusing (M² ~ 1) based on ABCD matrix propagation.

Q-switching stability limit calculator

Stability limit for passive Q-switching.

Ultrafast pulse propagation simulator

Nonlinear Schrödinger equation solver using the split-step Fourier method.

Material dispersion calculator

Refractive index, group delay, and GDD of optical materials.

Astigmatism compensation (concave mirror tilt)

Concave mirror tilting angle for astigmatism compensation in laser cavities.

Fresnel reflectance calculator

Interfacial reflectance between two media as a function of incident angle.

For thin-film metrologists

Prism coupler analyzer

Least-squares analysis of planar-waveguide modes for precise thin-film index and thickness.

Multilayer reflectance / transmittance

Transfer-matrix calculation for multilayer optical systems.

For general users

Character and word counter

Count characters and words in text.

x-y plotter with curve fitting

Plot data and fit multiple curve models.

Days calculator

Date differences and other date utilities.

Data extractor from graph

Digitize data points from a graph image.
