Split-Step Fourier Method Simulator

Numerical solution of the Nonlinear Schrödinger Equation for femtosecond pulse propagation

Simulation Parameters

Program Description

1. Nonlinear Schrödinger Equation (NLSE)

∂A/∂z + (α/2)A + i(β₂/2)(∂²A/∂T²) = iγ|A|²A
This simulator solves the Nonlinear Schrödinger Equation (NLSE) which describes the propagation of optical pulses in nonlinear dispersive media such as optical fibers and waveguides. The equation governs how the complex envelope A(z,T) of an optical pulse evolves as it propagates along the z-direction.
Parameters in the NLSE:
  • A(z,T): Complex envelope of the optical field amplitude
  • z: Propagation distance along the waveguide/fiber
  • T: Time in the moving frame (T = t - z/vg)
  • α: Linear loss coefficient (dB/km) - accounts for material absorption and scattering losses
  • β₂: Group velocity dispersion (GVD) parameter (s²/m) - describes pulse broadening due to chromatic dispersion
  • γ: Nonlinear coefficient (W⁻¹m⁻¹) - quantifies the strength of the Kerr nonlinearity

2. Initial Conditions (Sech² Pulse)

A(0,T) = √P₀ × sech(T/T₀) × exp[-i(C/2)(T/T₀)²]
The initial pulse is modeled as a hyperbolic secant (sech) pulse, which is the characteristic shape of a fundamental soliton. The pulse width parameter T₀ is related to the FWHM by: FWHM = 2 × acosh(√2) × T₀ ≈ 1.763 × T₀.

2. Initial Conditions (Gaussian Pulse)

A(0,T) = √P₀ × exp[-(1+iC)/2 × (T/T₀)²]
The initial pulse is modeled as a chirped Gaussian pulse. The pulse width parameter T₀ is related to the FWHM by: FWHM = 2 × √(ln(2)) × T₀ ≈ 1.665 × T₀.
Initial Condition Parameters:
  • P₀: Initial peak power (W), calculated from Average Power and Repetition Rate.
  • T₀: Pulse width parameter (s), related to the FWHM duration.
  • C: Chirp parameter, describing the initial frequency sweep. C=0 is a transform-limited pulse.
